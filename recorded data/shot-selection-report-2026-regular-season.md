# Shot Selection Report — 2026 Regular Season

**Brighton Varsity · 12 official games · 201 shots · prepared Oct 6, 2026**

## The question

Are we too willing to shoot from distance? Would we score more by working the ball into the box, even if it costs us a few shots per game?

## The short answer

**Yes.** A shot from inside the box is worth about **3 shots from outside it**. Right now more than half our shots come from outside the box, and those shots produce fewer than a third of our goals.

The catch: the box only pays off when the ball **arrives by a pass or cross**. Dribbling into the box for a forced shot is no better than shooting from 25 yards.

---

## 1. Where our goals come from

| Shot location | Shots | Goals | Scoring rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Inside the box | 95 | 15 | **16%** |
| Outside the box | 106 | 6 | **6%** |
| Within 9 yards of goal | 21 | 8 | **38%** |
| 27+ yards out | 71 | 3 | **4%** |

- **53%** of our shots are from outside the box. They produced **29%** of our goals.
- About 1 in 6 box shots goes in. About 1 in 18 outside shots goes in.
- The difference is statistically meaningful, not luck. Resampled game by game, the box comes out ahead about 97% of the time.

## 2. It's distance, not finishing

Our shooting accuracy is about the same from everywhere:

| | On target | On-target shots that score |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Inside the box | 51% | 31% |
| Outside the box | 45% | 12% |

We hit the frame from distance almost as often as we do up close. Those shots are simply easy saves. Our goal totals line up almost exactly with what distance alone predicts, so this isn't a finishing problem we can coach away. Long shots score at the rate long shots usually score.

**Approximate scoring chance by distance (our data):**

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Chance of a goal | 28% | 22% | 16% | 12% | 9% | 6% | 4.5% | 3% |

## 3. How the box shot is created matters most

| Box shots | Shots | Goals | Scoring rate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| After a pass, cross, or through ball | 39 | 11 | **28%** |
| Unassisted (dribble in, scramble) | 56 | 4 | **7%** |

**This is the key coaching point.** An unassisted box shot scores at about the same rate as a shot from outside. The value comes from arriving in the box on the end of a pass or cross, not just from being in the box.

*(Caveat: if assists weren't logged consistently in every game, this gap may be somewhat overstated.)*

## 4. The trade-off: fewer shots, more goals?

Today we average **7.9 box shots + 8.8 outside shots = 16.8 shots per game**, which yields **1.75 goals per game** (not counting penalties).

What happens if we pass up some long shots and work the ball instead:

| We pass up… | …and this many become box shots | Shots per game | Goals per game |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Nothing (today) | — | 16.8 | 1.75 |
| Half our long shots | 1 in 3 | 13.9 | 1.75 (even) |
| Half our long shots | 1 in 2 | 14.5 | **1.85** |
| Half our long shots | All of them | 16.8 | **2.20** |

**Break-even rule:** if about 1 in 3 passed-up long shots turns into a box shot, we score the same while taking ~3 fewer shots per game. Anything better than that is a net gain.

**Safe margin:** the estimate is rough, but even under cautious assumptions we come out ahead if we give up no more than about **1½ long shots for every extra box shot** we create.

## What this suggests for practice and games

1. **Make the extra pass.** Treat a shot from 25+ yards as a last option unless the shooter is truly open with a clear look.
2. **Get to the 6–12 yard zone.** Crosses, cutbacks, and through balls into that area produce our best chances (38% from inside 9 yards).
3. **Don't confuse "in the box" with "a good chance."** A forced dribble-in shot is worth about the same as a long one. The pass that creates the shot is what matters.
4. **Talk with our high-volume long shooters** about recycling the ball instead of hitting it from range, especially players who also finish well in the box.

## Limits of this analysis

- **Small sample.** 21 goals in 12 games; the outside-the-box rate is based on just 6 goals.
- **Not a controlled comparison.** Some long shots happen because nothing better was available. Passing them up might sometimes mean losing the ball, which is why the break-even math matters more than the raw 3-to-1 ratio.
- **Hidden value isn't captured.** Rebounds or corners won off long shots aren't fully reflected in the data.
- Excludes one penalty, one shot with a bad location, and corner/foul entries. Including the preseason Morgan game gives the same picture.
