# Scheduling priorities to maximize final MaxPreps ranking

For Ryan. Brighton FS girls, 5A. Current: #9 in 5A, rating 14.55.

## Read this first: our actual problem, from today's list

We are 9-2 and ranked #9. Our rating is 14.55. Our "Str" (strength of
schedule) is **3.8 -- the lowest of any team in the top 12.** Every team
ranked above us except one has a tougher schedule than we do.

That is the whole story. Our record is not the problem. A 9-2 record is
good. The problem is that the rating thinks our wins were cheap, because
the teams we beat are weak.

The clearest proof is sitting in the same list:

- **Lone Peak is 8-3 and ranked #5** (rating 17.87), above us at 9-2,
  because its Str is 13.5. Three losses, still rated well above us,
  because it played a monster schedule.
- **Green Canyon is 11-0 and only #3** (Str 1.5), behind two teams that
  have losses -- Davis (8-1) and Crimson Cliffs (9-1). Undefeated did not
  buy them #1, because their schedule was soft.
- **Manti (11-1) and Box Elder (10-1)** are stuck at #11 and #14 with
  near-zero Str. Great records, cheap schedules, capped ratings.

So: raising our rating means raising the quality of who we play. That is
the one lever we have not been pulling.

## The priorities, ranked by influence on final ranking

### 1. Beat good teams. This is the single biggest lever.

A win over a top-15 opponent moves us more than anything else on this
list. The rating is essentially asking "how strong must Brighton be to
have beaten the teams it beat?" -- and only strong opponents force a high
answer. We control this through who we schedule out of region and by
winning those games.

### 2. Do not lose to weak teams. This is the biggest downside risk.

An unexpected loss is the most expensive single result available to us,
and it gets more expensive the weaker the opponent. Losing 1-0 to a
bottom-half team costs far more than losing 1-0 to a top-5 team. We are
good enough that this mostly means: take every game seriously, do not
rest everyone against a cupcake and get surprised.

### 3. Schedule UP in non-region games. Add tough opponents.

Our Str is 3.8 and it needs to climb. The only place we can move it is the
non-region schedule, because region is fixed. Concretely, for next year
and for any non-region games still open this year:

- Target teams rated **roughly top-20 in the state**, across any
  classification.
- A competitive **loss to a genuinely strong team barely hurts us**, and
  a win over one is the best result we can get. The risk is asymmetric in
  our favor. MaxPreps says this outright: a close loss to a much
  higher-rated team can actually raise your rating.
- Two or three of these, not a whole schedule of them. We still need to
  win most of our games.

### 4. Classification is irrelevant. Stop thinking in 4A/5A/6A.

The rating never uses classification. We scheduled 4A teams this year as
if that were "playing down" -- but **Crimson Cliffs (4A) is ranked #1 in
the entire state and Green Canyon (4A) is #3.** A game against the top of
4A is a far better rating asset than a game against the bottom of 6A. The
only question that matters is the opponent's rating number, not its
class. Schedule by the rating list, full stop.

### 5. Do NOT run up the score. It does nothing past 5 goals.

Soccer has a 5-goal cap in this system. UHSAA says plainly that running up
scores will not improve a rating. A 9-0 win and a 5-0 win are worth
exactly the same. Everything past the fifth goal is pure downside --
injuries, and goodwill with programs we have to schedule again next year.
Win by five, empty the bench, move on.

### 6. Report scores fast and correctly, especially out-of-state ones.

The rating is only as good as the data in MaxPreps. Two specifics:

- If we ever play an out-of-state team, it is OUR job to make sure their
  scores get entered. An opponent with missing games has a wrong rating,
  which makes our Str wrong.
- Enter non-varsity games by selecting "Non-Varsity Opponent" so they do
  not pollute the varsity graph.

### 7. Prefer opponents who will be good LATER, not just now.

About half of what moves our rating from here is what our past opponents
do the rest of the season. A team we beat in August who turns into a
November power gets that win re-credited to us at full value. When choosing
between two similar opponents, take the one trending up.

## The one thing we cannot change, and why it matters

Our region -- Bountiful, Olympus, Brighton, Viewmont, Woods Cross, Alta,
Skyline, each played twice -- is locked. That is actually a mixed bag for
our Str:

- Olympus (#7, rating 16.27) and Bountiful (#10, 13.98) are genuinely
  strong. Those four games (twice each) are helping us.
- Alta (rating -2.96), Woods Cross (3.56), Viewmont (8.10), and Skyline
  (4.85) are dragging our Str down, and we cannot avoid them.

That is exactly why the non-region schedule has to do the heavy lifting.
Half our games are fixed at a mediocre average strength. The only way to
pull our Str up is to make the other half count.

## If Ryan wants the one-sentence version

Play and beat the strongest teams we can find out of region, never lose to
a weak one, ignore what class anyone is in, and stop caring about the
scoreline once we are up five.
